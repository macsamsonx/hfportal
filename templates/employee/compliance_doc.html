Employee Handbook & Code of Conduct

Document No: HF-HR-001 · Version {{ doc.version }} · Effective Date: January 2025
Approved by: Management

📋 Filler Document: Upload your official Employee Handbook PDF via HR > Compliance Documents to replace this template.

1. Company Vision & Mission

Hundredfold Digital Marketing Services is committed to delivering excellent digital marketing solutions while upholding Christian values, integrity, and excellence in all our endeavors.

2. Core Values

  • Integrity — We act with honesty and transparency in all our dealings.
  • Excellence — We pursue the highest quality in everything we do.
  • Stewardship — We are responsible caretakers of our resources and relationships.
  • Teamwork — We collaborate and support one another toward shared goals.
  • Faith — We acknowledge God in all our work and decisions.

3. Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to conduct themselves professionally, honestly, and respectfully at all times — whether inside the workplace, while representing the company, or in digital communications.

  • Treat all colleagues, clients, and stakeholders with dignity and respect.
  • Protect confidential company and client information at all times.
  • Report any conflicts of interest promptly to your supervisor or HR.
  • Do not engage in harassment, discrimination, or any form of workplace violence.
  • Use company resources (internet, equipment, software) only for authorized work purposes.
  • Represent the company positively on social media and public platforms.

4. Working Hours & Attendance

Employees are expected to be punctual and to complete their assigned work hours. Chronic tardiness or unexplained absences are subject to disciplinary action in accordance with the Philippine Labor Code.

5. Leave Entitlements

Employees are entitled to leave benefits in accordance with Philippine law: Service Incentive Leave (RA 8187), Maternity Leave (RA 11210), Paternity Leave (RA 8187), Solo Parent Leave (RA 8972), and VAWC Leave (RA 9262), among others.

6. Data Privacy

The company collects and processes employee personal data in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). Employees have the right to access, correct, and object to processing of their personal data.

7. Disciplinary Procedures

The company follows due process in all disciplinary matters as required by the Labor Code. No employee shall be dismissed without proper cause and without being given the opportunity to be heard through a written Notice to Explain (NTE) and subsequent Notice of Decision (NOD).

8. Grievance Mechanism

Employees may raise concerns or grievances through their immediate supervisor, the HR Manager, or directly to management. All complaints will be handled promptly, confidentially, and impartially.

9. Acknowledgment

By signing below, the employee confirms that they have received, read, and understood the contents of this Employee Handbook and agree to abide by its policies and guidelines.

Sensitive Personal Data — Processing Consent

Under RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012), Section 13 — Sensitive Personal Information

The company collects and processes the following categories of sensitive personal information as defined under Section 3(l) of RA 10173. Your specific, informed, and freely given consent is required for each category below:

Categories of Sensitive Data Collected

  • Personality & Behavioral Profile — DISC Assessment results (temperament type: Dominant, Influential, Steady, Conscientious)

  • Emotional Intelligence — EQ Assessment scores across Self-Awareness, Self-Regulation, Motivation, Empathy, Social Skills

Retention & Your Rights

Data is retained for the duration of employment plus seven (7) years as required by law. You have the right to: access your personal data, correct inaccuracies, object to processing, and file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

OSHS Safety Orientation Acknowledgment

Document No: HF-HR-006 · Version {{ doc.version }}
DOLE OSHS Rule 1040 — Training and Personnel Development

📋 Filler Document: Upload your official OSHS Orientation PDF via HR > Compliance Documents to replace this template.

1. Workplace Safety Policy

Hundredfold Digital Marketing Services is committed to maintaining a safe, healthy, and hazard-free work environment for all employees, in compliance with DOLE OSHS (as amended by RA 11058) and DOLE Department Order 198-18.

2. Emergency Procedures

  • Fire: Activate the nearest fire alarm, evacuate via designated exit routes, do not use elevators, assemble at the designated muster point.
  • Earthquake: Duck, cover, and hold. Move away from windows. Do not run outside until shaking stops.
  • Medical Emergency: Call emergency services (911) and notify HR immediately. First aid kit location: Admin area.

3. Ergonomics & Remote Work Safety

For remote and home-based workers: ensure your workstation has adequate lighting, proper chair height, and monitor position to reduce ergonomic strain. Report any work-related injuries immediately to HR regardless of work location.

4. Reporting Hazards

Any employee who identifies a workplace hazard or safety concern is encouraged to report it immediately to HR or their direct supervisor. Reports may be made anonymously. Retaliation against employees for good-faith safety reports is prohibited.

5. Work-Related Injury Reporting

All work-related accidents, near-misses, or occupational illnesses must be reported to HR within 24 hours. HR will file the required DOLE WAIR (Work Accident/Illness Report) forms within the prescribed period.

6. Employee Safety Rights

Under RA 11058, all employees have the right to: refuse unsafe work without disciplinary consequences, request safety inspections, participate in OSHS programs, and receive safety training relevant to their role.

Workplace Monitoring Consent

Document No: HF-HR-007 · Version {{ doc.version }}
Under RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act) and NPC Circular 2023-01

1. Forms of Monitoring

The company may employ the following monitoring measures for legitimate business and security purposes:

  • CCTV Surveillance — Cameras may be installed in common areas, entrances/exits, and work areas. CCTV footage is retained for 30 days.
  • Digital Activity Logging — The employee portal logs: login timestamps, IP addresses, browser/device information, and all system actions for security and audit purposes.
  • Attendance Tracking — Clock-in/clock-out times and IP addresses are recorded to verify attendance and work hours.
  • Remote Work Monitoring — For WFH employees, the system may log connection times and work output data.

2. Purpose of Monitoring

Monitoring is conducted solely for: employee safety and security, prevention of theft or data breaches, verification of work hours, compliance with client and regulatory requirements, and investigation of incidents when required.

3. Limitations

Monitoring is conducted proportionally and only to the extent necessary for the stated purposes. Private areas (restrooms, prayer rooms) are never monitored. CCTV footage is accessible only by authorized HR and Management personnel.

4. Data Subject Rights

You may request access to records about yourself, request correction of inaccurate records, or file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission at www.privacy.gov.ph.

5. Your Consent

By signing below, you acknowledge that you have been informed of, and consent to, the monitoring measures described above as a condition of employment, pursuant to RA 10173 and NPC Circular 2023-01.
